🥗 **YIELD**
 
**Track Groceries, Reduce Waste & Cook Smarter**

Every year, millions of tonnes of food end up in landfills, accelerating climate change and wasting household budgets. **Yield** is a personal kitchen assistant designed to help users organize their groceries, eliminate food waste, discover recipes based on existing ingredients, and track consumption habits over time.

✨ **Key Features**

**Virtual Fridge**: Log groceries easily with categories, item names, quantities, and expiration dates to maintain an accurate digital inventory.

**Smart Expiration Alerts:** Automatic notifications trigger when items are within 3 days of expiring, prompting timely usage.

**Ingredient-Based Recipe Suggestions:** Select any ingredient in your virtual fridge to instantly fetch and discover matching recipes.

**Waste & Consumption Dashboard:** Visualize weekly food habits with interactive charts comparing consumed vs. wasted food over time.

**Food Trivia & Facts:** Interactive quizzes and randomized food facts built in to engage users on sustainability.
