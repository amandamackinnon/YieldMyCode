Waste Less. Cook Smarter.

Turn the groceries you already have into meals you'll love. Track expiry dates, discover recipes, explore food trivia, and see your waste-saving progress.

Make every grocery count with Yield, the kitchen companion that helps you stay organized, use food in time, and find inspiration for your next meal.

YOUR FRIDGE, AT A GLANCE

Add groceries with a name, category, quantity, unit, and expiry date. Your virtual fridge keeps everything organized so you always know what is on hand.

USE IT BEFORE YOU LOSE IT

Yield highlights items that are close to expiry, helping you choose what to use next and prevent good food from being forgotten.

COOK WITH WHAT YOU HAVE

Select an ingredient to explore recipes that feature it. Turn the food already in your kitchen into meal inspiration.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS

Mark items as consumed or wasted, then view weekly activity and food-waste charts. Understand your habits and build a more mindful kitchen routine.

MAKE FOOD FUN

Discover ingredient facts and test your knowledge with multiple-choice food trivia.

From planning dinner to checking what needs attention today, Yield makes it easier to stay on top of your groceries, waste less, and cook smarter.
